AROHA — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

AROHA ("we," "our," or "us") provides a personalized conversational companion experience. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and display name (provided via Sign in with Apple, Google, or email registration).
  • Phone Number: Used exclusively to facilitate AI-initiated voice calls if you opt-in to this feature.
  • Chat & Voice Messages: Text conversations and transcribed audio from voice calls between you and your AI partner, used to provide contextual responses.
  • Personality Preferences: Partner traits and settings you configure.
  • Device Token: Expo Push Notification token for sending notifications.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the core app experience — generating AI responses, remembering conversation context, and sending notifications. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Clerk: Authentication (Sign in with Apple, Google).
  • Groq & xAI: AI language models for generating conversational responses.
  • Twilio & Amazon Polly: Processing and routing of phone calls and speech-to-text generation.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and payment management.
  • Expo: Push notification delivery.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app. Upon deletion, all chat messages, profile information, and partner data are permanently removed from our servers.

6. Subscriptions

AROHA offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Manage your subscription in your device's App Store settings. See our Terms of Use for full subscription details.
